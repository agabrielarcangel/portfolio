How I Work

From Build to Sign-Off

A repeatable QA pass. Every ticket moves through the same five stages, from reading the intent of the change to the sign-off a team can ship on.

01
📋
Understand
Read the ticket and the design, and agree what done looks like.
02
🧭
Walk The Flow
Run the journey end to end, not just the screen that changed.
03
🔎
Inspect
Layout, spacing, copy, motion and states, down to the pixel.
04
🐞
Report
Steps, screenshot, expected and actual, and the likely cause.
05
✅
Verify
Retest the fix, check nothing nearby broke, then sign it off.
